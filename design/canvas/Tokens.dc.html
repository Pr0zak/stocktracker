CHART MARKS · WHERE SHAPE DOES THE WORK
Candles: hollow up, filled down
Histograms: a signed baseline
Where a second channel is needed and where it is not. Candles, histogram bars, seasonality bars and heat-map tiles get shape or a signed baseline, because nothing near them states the direction. A price line does not: the signed figure with its ▲/▼ sits directly above it in the same card, so a solid line in the semantic ink is honest. Reference lines — cost, previous close, the benchmark — are always neutral and dashed, because a reference is not a verdict.
Chart annotations, plot notes and axis ticks come off the hardcoded 8sp to an 11sp floor and honour fontScale; the heat map's division by fontScale is deleted outright.

Counted 11 Sep 2026 over app/src/main/java/com/stocktracker/app, excluding ui/theme. A CI lint gate on Color(0x outside the theme is what stops the anarchy growing back; without it this release is undone by the next feature.
Dark only. Dropping the light scheme closes the audit's top finding outright rather than repainting it: the pastel gain at 1.66:1 on the light surface stops existing. It also removes the status-bar bug, since enableEdgeToEdge no longer has an in-app theme to disagree with.
Every ratio here is computed against the surface it is drawn on. The design sheets' own light inks were checked too — #2E9E57 is 3.10:1 on #F2F4F7 and the pair separates by only 1.47 — which is one more reason the light scheme goes rather than ships half-fixed.
