Label degradation

a label either FITS or is not drawn

Truncating a ticker does not abbreviate it, it renames it — GOOGL cut to four characters is GOOG, a different real security that is on the same screen. So there is no ellipsis rule for tickers; there is a fit test. Only the company name, which is a disambiguator rather than an identifier, may ellipsize.

Why the name moved rather than went away. It disambiguates tickers people do not know by heart, which is most of them outside the top twenty. On its own line it cost a full row of height on every row in the list; beside the ticker it costs nothing extra, because the ticker line had slack to the right in every case. Open question: the sparkline is the widest optional element at 58px. On a small phone in landscape it is the first thing worth dropping, and there is currently no breakpoint that does so — the row simply gets tighter around it.
